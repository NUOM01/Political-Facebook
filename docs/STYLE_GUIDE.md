# Nepal Political Campaign Writing Style Guide & Messaging Playbook

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Identity
This style guide governs all content, Facebook ad copies, video scripts, and image captions generated across our autonomous agent ecosystem for political intelligence and media operations in Nepal (with a specific lens on Rastriya Swatantra Party / Rabi Lamichhane and progressive reformist messaging).

Our primary strategic objective is **high-converting engagement, narrative authority, and emotional resonance** across urban, rural, and international Nepali diaspora demographics on Meta platforms.

---

## 2. Core Pillars of the Voice
The campaign voice is built on three pillars that reflect modern Nepali political dynamics:

### Pillar 1: Direct Accountability & Grassroots Anger (जनगुनासो र प्रत्यक्ष जवाफदेहिता)
- **Focus:** Fighting systemic stagnation, bureaucratic red tape, passport/license line misery, public fund embezzlement, and old-guard political cartels.
- **Tone:** Unapologetic, firm, deeply empathetic with everyday citizen frustration, yet constructive and reform-oriented.
- **Rhetorical Style:** Rhetorical questions, stark contrasts ("वर्षौंदेखिको सिन्डिकेट vs नागरिकको अधिकार"), clear pledges.

### Pillar 2: Meritocracy & Transparent Governance (सुशासन र योग्यता प्रणाली)
- **Focus:** Capable professionals over party loyalists, transparent budgets, merit-based civil service, digital governance, open tender systems.
- **Tone:** Modern, data-backed, confident, forward-thinking.
- **Target Audience:** Urban voters, educated youth, professionals, entrepreneurs.

### Pillar 3: Youth Hope & Diaspora Dignity (युवा रोजगारी र परदेशको पीडा)
- **Focus:** Halting forced youth migration, Gulf/Malaysia worker dignity, remittance protection, enabling returnees to build businesses in Nepal.
- **Tone:** Emotive, unifying, patriotic without chauvinism ("देश फर्किने आधार बनाऔं").

## 3. Linguistic Framework & Formats

### A. Language Mix
1. **Primary: Conversational & Formal Devanagari (शुद्ध तथा जनबोलीको नेपाली):**
   - Natural phrasing rather than overly archaic Sanskritized vocabulary.
   - Use universally understood idioms and culturally resonant metaphors (e.g., "सिन्डिकेट तोड्ने", "घण्टीको आवाज", "परिवर्तनको पक्षमा").
2. **Secondary: Romanized Hooks & Hybrid English (for Tech/Youth targeting):**
   - Use punchy Romanized Nepali for the first 1-2 lines on mobile ads where instant readability stops scrolling (e.g., *"Hamro aawaz, hamro adhikar"*).
   - English summaries for international diaspora communities and policy whitepapers.

---

## 4. The 3-Second Facebook Ad Anatomy (Mobile Optimized)

Every Facebook post and sponsored ad must strictly follow this 4-tier conversion structure:

```
[ Tier 1: The Scroll-Stopper Hook ] (1-2 lines)
  ⚡ Emotive question, shocking stat, or bold declaration that calls out a specific pain point.
  
[ Tier 2: The Core Conflict / Agitation ] (2-3 lines)
  📌 Contrast the current failed reality with the citizen's lived hardship (corruption, delay, neglect).
  
[ Tier 3: The Solution / Reform Commitment ] (3-4 bullet points)
  ✅ Concrete policy action, bill, parliamentary initiative, or community action plan.
  
[ Tier 4: Clear Call-To-Action (CTA) ] (1-2 lines)
  👉 Vote, comment, take our survey, share to your group, or watch full speech.
```

## 5. Exemplar Ad Copy Frameworks

### Example A: Anti-Corruption & Public Service Reform
```text
🚨 ५ घण्टा लाइन बस्दा पनि काम नहुने यो अवस्था कहिलेसम्म?

सरकारी कार्यालयमा सेवा लिन जाँदा जनताले भोग्नुपरेको सास्ती कुनै नयाँ कुरा होइन। बिचौलिया बिना काम नबन्ने, फाइल महिनौंसम्म रोकिने र आफ्नै अधिकार लिन पनि चाकडी गर्नुपर्ने सिन्डिकेट अब तोडिनैपर्छ।

हाम्रो स्पष्ट अडान र कार्ययोजना:
🔹 सम्पूर्ण सार्वजनिक सेवालाई डिजिटल र टाइम-बाउन्ड बनाउने
🔹 सेवाग्राहीलाई अनावश्यक ढिलासुस्ती गराउनेमाथि तत्काल कारबाही
🔹 सुशासन र नागरिक सन्तुष्टिलाई नै मूल्यांकनको मुख्य आधार बनाउने

परिवर्तन भाषणले होइन, इमानदार कामले आउँछ।

👇 तपाईंले हालै सरकारी सेवा लिँदा कस्तो अनुभव गर्नुभयो? कमेन्टमा आफ्नो राय राख्नुहोस् र नागरिक सर्वेक्षणमा भाग लिनुहोस्।
```

## 6. Guardrails & Prohibited Tactics
- ❌ **No unsubstantiated personal attacks:** Criticize policies, track records, governance failures, and systemic abuse—never engage in unverified personal smears.
- ❌ **No communal, ethnic, or regional polarization:** Nepal's strength lies in unity. All provinces (Koshi, Madhesh, Bagmati, Gandaki, Lumbini, Karnali, Sudurpashchim) must be represented with equal respect.
- ❌ **Strict Fact-Checking:** Every statistic (inflation, national debt, ad spend, survey results) cited in copies or infographics must be traceable to our verified database (`campaign.db`) or recognized public sources.
- ❌ **No Clickbait Deception:** The headline hook must accurately deliver on the content of the attached image or video.
